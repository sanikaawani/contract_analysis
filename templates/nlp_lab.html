Technique 6: Part-of-Speech (POS) Tagging & Modal Obligation Scoring

Tags lexical tokens using Penn Treebank tags. Modal verbs (MD) are specifically analyzed to distinguish between mandatory legal obligations (shall/must) and discretionary liberties (may).

POS Tag Distribution
Modal Verb Legal Weightings
  • shall / must: Strict Mandatory Covenant (High Duty)
  • will / agree to: Future Binding Commitment (Medium Duty)
  • may / can: Discretionary Permissive Liberty (Optional)
