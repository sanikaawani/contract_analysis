8 Evaluated Contract Risk Categories

Trained on Indian commercial contracts and statutory case law benchmarks

Test on sample agreement
1. Automatic Renewal

Detects evergreen rollover clauses, narrow non-renewal windows (<15 days), and unannounced price hikes.

2. Payment Terms

Flags Net 90–120 day delays, unilateral withholding, subjective milestones, and 24% penal interest on SMBs.

3. Liability Clauses

Identifies uncapped indemnity, loss of profits exposure, and lack of reciprocal liability caps.

4. Penalty Clauses

Evaluates liquidated damages (2% per day), bank guarantee forfeitures, and Section 74 Indian Contract Act limits.

5. Termination Clauses

Flags 24-hr unilateral termination for convenience, zero compensation for work-in-progress, and 3-year lock-ins.

6. Confidentiality Clauses

Checks perpetual non-disclosure, ex-parte injunction risks, and missing standard trade secret carve-outs.

7. Non-Compete Clauses

Flags post-termination trade restraints (void under Section 27 of Indian Contract Act 1872) and broad non-solicits.

8. Restrictive Clauses

Detects full forfeiture of background IP, unannounced physical/code audits, and negative public marketing covenants.
